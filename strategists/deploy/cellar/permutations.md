# Permutations
The main functionalities of a cellar are implemented in [**Cellar.sol**](https://github.com/PeggyJV/cellar-contracts/blob/main/src/base/Cellar.sol). However typically a strategists will choose to deploy one of the permutations of the base cellar contract located in [**src/base/permutations**](https://github.com/PeggyJV/cellar-contracts/tree/main/src/base/permutations).

Building blocks:
- AaveFlashLoans
- BalancerFlashLoans
- MultiAssetDeposit
- NativeSupport
- Oracle
- ShareLockPeriod
